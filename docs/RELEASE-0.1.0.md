# TF2Do 0.1.0

The first TF2Do release gives biotechnology students and researchers a place to organize the work around a project. Capture the next task, set aside a day to work on it, and keep the notes you’ll need nearby.

- **Plan your day and week.** Planned days and deadlines are separate. Overdue work stays visible.
- **Keep related work together.** Organize tasks in projects, attach plain-text notes, and search across the workspace.
- **Keep a copy of your work.** Export the workspace as JSON and preview what will be restored before replacing anything.

To try it, follow the setup in the [README](../README.md) and choose **Load demo workspace**. Version 0.1.0 saves in your browser. Use an export to move work between browsers or devices.

Developed and maintained by **Robert Waite**, a biotechnology graduate student at the University of South Florida.
